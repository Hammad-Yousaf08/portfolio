About Me

From Traditional DevOps to
Multi-Cloud & MLOps

Over the past 2.5 years, I've built and maintained production infrastructure serving real users across SaaS platforms, ERP systems, and blockchain applications. My journey started with traditional cloud deployments on AWS, then expanded into multi-cloud environments including Azure, GCP, and Alibaba Cloud.

I've deployed ERPNext for Middle East clients, built multi-environment setups for an HSE SaaS platform, and architected infrastructure for the PlayDeFi blockchain project. Along the way, I gained hands-on experience with ML model deployments, adding MLOps capabilities to my DevOps foundation.

What drives me is solving real infrastructure challenges—whether it's designing VPC architecture with proper subnet isolation, implementing CI/CD pipelines that reduce deployment time by 70%, or building event-driven workflows using Lambda and EventBridge. I focus on security, high availability, and cost optimization.
